## Accordions & FAQs

Collapsible disclosure panels for FAQs and secondary content:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Raindrop and Cyclone-serve?">
    **Raindrop** is the primary observation storage engine and station registry, while **Cyclone-serve** is a high-speed tiled raster server designed specifically for numerical weather model fields (GRIB2) and thermodynamic calculations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How often do mesonet stations update?">
    CSSL-owned mesonet stations report observations every 60 seconds over cellular LTE networks. External network partners (ECCC, provincial agencies) update on 10 to 60 minute schedules.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use CSSL data in commercial software?">
    Yes! CSSL observations and model tiles can be integrated into research, educational, and commercial applications. Please review the licensing guidelines in the Weather Intelligence portal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
