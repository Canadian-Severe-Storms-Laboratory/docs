The **Canadian Severe Storms Laboratory (CSSL)** Weather Intelligence Portal is Canada's central repository and access point for high-resolution surface weather observations, numerical forecast model tiles, and severe storm analytics.

<Callout type="tip" title="CSSL Weather Intelligence Environment">
  Live production maps and high-frequency real-time observation feeds are available at [weather.cssl.ca](https://weather.cssl.ca). All API keys can be managed via the Weather Intelligence settings dashboard.

## Core Capabilities

- **Dense Multi-Network Observations**: Ingests observations across Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC), NOAA, DWD, provincial mesonet networks, and CSSL research stations.
- **Tiled Cyclone Model Grids**: High-speed raster tiles for HRRR, RAP, RDPS, and GDPS forecast products with rotated and Lambert conformal projections.
- **Sub-Minute Radar & Lightning Overlay**: WebGL-accelerated visualization powered by Deck.gl and MapLibre.
- **CAP Alert Streaming**: Real-time Common Alerting Protocol warnings, watches, and advisories mapped with geospatial geometry.
